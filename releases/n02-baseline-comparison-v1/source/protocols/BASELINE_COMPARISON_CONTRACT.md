# Baseline comparison contract

Status: frozen implementation contract; this file does not imply a pilot, Gate 2, formal run, or Gate 3 pass.

## Methods and clocks

The primary comparison contains Greedy, Auction, CBBA-lite, PPO, GPPO, Periodic-GPPO, Event-GPPO, and Event-GPPO-WM. Periodic-GPPO-WM is an auxiliary ninth control. Greedy, Auction, CBBA-lite, PPO, and GPPO make a planning decision each physical minute; periodic methods decide at minutes divisible by four; event methods decide only when the shared frozen event trigger fires. Every method still runs public detection and affected-scope calculation at every physical tick. A non-decision tick executes an explicit legal NOOP and makes no candidate-generation, actor, or planning-time world-model call. Critic value collection during training may run each physical tick.

The rule baselines are candidate-constrained implementations over the shared legal catalogue. Split and cooperate operations are not utility-projected by the current Greedy, Auction, or CBBA-lite selector; every rule receipt reports supported and unsupported legal-candidate counts, supported fraction, and unsupported candidate types. CBBA-lite is an ordered-bundle, connected-component consensus approximation over representable catalogue pairs. It is not claimed as full CBBA or as an equivalent optimum to a traditional unrestricted baseline. Gate2 must adjudicate whether this candidate constraint weakens the comparison before the rule methods are described as traditional baselines.

## Shared world, scope, and catalogues

Each episode uses the same six frozen 100x100 parents, initial C1 assignment, event tape, exogenous identity, four UAVs, two helicopters, twelve original task roots, and 60-minute horizon. The six parents remain split into four training and two development parents. Empty affected domains are NOOP-only. Local and global eligibility use the same public affected-domain mask and actual `current_assignment_feasible` result for every method. An infeasible current assignment or registered `global_required` reason authorizes global planning; the periodic clock alone never does. A global action with no real assignment/order effect still incurs the global invocation charge and is not an effective reconstruction.

For identical public observation, history, scope, and candidate cap, all methods use the shared generator and receive the identical ordered catalogue and hash. Catalogues can differ after closed-loop states diverge. No duplicate actions are added to fill the maximum of 16. Repair, legality, and NOOP handling are shared.

## Learning and world model

PPO and GPPO share public inputs, the 631-feature candidate representation, categorical action head, critic heads, reward, repair, optimizer settings, and update budgets. PPO uses a flat encoder and GPPO uses typed relational message passing. WM variants use the same frozen ensemble only as a detached auxiliary-critic input. WM predictions never affect actor logits, catalogues, candidate ordering, scope, or trigger decisions. Each training episode records all physical transitions for critic/GAE; actor loss uses decision rows only. Minibatch tails are retained. The update cap is cumulative over all rounds for a method and seed, while all prescribed training episodes still run after the cap is reached.

Pilot uses seed 8201, four training episodes (one round), four PPO epochs, minibatch 32, and at most 32 optimizer steps per learning method. Formal uses seeds 8201, 8202, and 8203, four rounds of four training episodes, the same epochs and minibatch size, and at most 128 cumulative steps per learning method and seed. Fresh WM and policy checkpoints are trained separately for pilot and formal profiles. Checkpoints bind source, config, data, trigger, and WM identities and restore optimizer plus Python/NumPy/Torch/CUDA/minibatch RNG state in a new process.

## Outcomes and resource accounting

All task outcomes are aggregated to the twelve original root tasks. Split children never enter denominators separately. Completion is a root task completed by the 60-minute horizon; a split root is complete only when every required child workload is complete. On-time means the root completes by its frozen deadline. Unreleased, failed, expired, and cancelled roots are not completed or on-time. Mean delay is the sum of positive completion lateness for completed roots and positive horizon/deadline exposure for incomplete non-cancelled roots, divided by twelve; cancelled roots accrue delay only until cancellation. Criticality is frozen from each original root's reset-time priority, with priority at least 5.0. Later deadline changes affect on-time and delay only; they do not redefine criticality. A critical root fails if it is not completed by the horizon. These definitions use no outcome-dependent normalization.

Effective reconstruction is a real task reassignment or queue-order change caused by a repair operation or a delivered command. Natural queue-head dispatch, task completion, queue popping, progress, and other autonomous simulator changes do not count. Delayed command delivery is attributed to its originating action through the delivery receipt and is counted when it actually changes assignment/order. Requested non-NOOP actions, local/global invocations, owner changes, queue reorder changes, and effective reconstruction are reported separately.

Environment utility includes simulator completion, delay, energy, risk, communication, and switching costs. Environment action-delivery messages and rule-protocol negotiation messages are reported separately; delivery communication is not charged twice. Net utility is environment utility minus 0.01 per rule-protocol message. Planning latency is reported separately and has zero penalty. `trigger_count` and `planning_invocation_count` count method clock ticks on which planning was invoked, even if only NOOP was eligible. `scope_nonempty_planning_count` counts planned invocations with affected task or platform IDs. `actionable_catalog_planning_count` and its legacy alias `nonempty_planning_invocation_count` count planned invocations whose legal candidate catalogue contains a non-NOOP action. `requested_nonnoop_count` separately counts chosen non-NOOP actions. `detector_event_edge_count` reports the shared detector independently. CUDA policy response measurements synchronize the device. Simulator communication delay is distinct from policy computation latency.

## Frozen Gate Thresholds

For Event-GPPO versus Periodic-GPPO and Event-GPPO-WM versus Periodic-GPPO-WM, the mean environment global utility difference must be at least zero; critical failures must not increase; completion and on-time rates may each fall by at most 0.02; and event planning invocations must be lower. At least one of environment action-delivery messages, rule-protocol messages, effective reconstructions, or reconstruction size must fall. Empty-domain global actions must equal zero, and event methods must show a raw local-repair witness. Net utility is reported as a secondary metric and does not replace environment global utility. Formal 3-seed by 2-development-parent cells are paired descriptive observations; they do not support a statistical-significance claim. A failed threshold is reported as a valid negative result and is never tuned against development outcomes.

## Required action receipts

Each physical tick records the public observation before/after, event detection and affected scope, feasibility result and reason, decision clock, explicit NOOP flag, ordered candidate catalogue and recomputable digest when planning, mask, selected candidate, complete action, repair receipt, operation and communication delivery receipts, action-linked command application receipts, queue/owner endpoints, raw leaf task/spec/platform state before and after, root mapping and split manifests, raw root endpoint task/platform records, reward terms and frozen reward formula/hash, environment and protocol message counts, policy/world-model forward counts, synchronized response time, and physical state changes. Operation receipts retain `operation`, `before_queues`, `after_queues`, and `owners_before`; command effects retain `originating_action_id`, delivery status, and actual owner/order effects. Raw leaf states permit an independent standard-library auditor to reconstruct root outcomes and reward components without importing production metrics. Production summaries are labeled `baseline-production-metrics/1` and are not the independent oracle.
