1. Preserve v1 and run six formal parents on exactly the v1 event tapes, with the 100x100 map contract. Separate mechanism diagnostics must have their own recomputed tape identities.
2. Calibrate tau_on at q=.75 from training data only. Bind its source hash and derive tau_off=tau_on*(1-.05). M1/M3/M4 consume the appropriate training manifest; absolute configuration overrides are rejected.
3. Execution residuals require consecutive high-threshold observations. Every lower score or other event clears the counter. External disturbances follow threshold/warmup/cooldown rules independently of residual persistence; strong events retain their bypass.
4. Replay M0-M2 under all three declared seeds with frozen inputs. These deterministic environment replays are explicitly not WM/PPO training or evidence of GPU execution.
5. NOOP remains available. All event and periodic rows select an allowed action under final catalog eligibility and mode authorization; missing feasible local candidates do not automatically authorize global replanning.
6. Audit actual M1 final catalogs and attempted-candidate rejections, and every M2 candidate-table row. Record legal/illegal totals, reasoned rejections and missing/invalid status. Missing data cannot pass.
7. Record local-repair assignments and queues before/after, affected scope and effective mutation. Separate train, development and diagnostic provenance. Requested counts alone cannot prove mechanism execution. At least one audited formal Event-G local mutation in train or development is required; report the development count separately without changing scenarios to force one.
8. Preserve independent raw-trace recomputation of NOOP and non-target labels, shared-prefix/tape binding, public-only C1 generation, physical 2x2 causal checks and training/development separation.
9. Evaluate task utility/quality and each reconstruction cost separately. Report Event-G-WM versus Event-G as a combined trigger-and-critic descriptive comparison, never isolated WM/critic gain or statistical significance.
10. GPU training, strict fresh-process restores, real PPO updates and frozen-WM auxiliary critic require new GPU receipts and independent Sol High review. Missing evidence yields REPAIR_REQUIRED; do not tune formal scenarios or thresholds using development outcomes.
